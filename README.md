# Pokémon RPS: Charizard vs. Venusaur vs. Blastoise
> A classic Rock-Paper-Scissors showdown featuring the Kanto starters!

## Authorship
- **Author:** Brandon Harwell
- **Date:** October 13 2026
- **Version:** 1.0.1

## User Story
- **As a** visitor to your GitHub profile,
- **I want** an interactive Pokémon battle web game,
- **So that** I have something simple and fun to do while looking around your page!

## Narrative
Step into the Kanto arena as a Pokémon Trainer! Choose your starter—Charizard (Fire), Venusaur (Grass), or Blastoise (Water)—and battle against the AI. Fire beats Grass, Grass beats Water, and Water beats Fire. Features both a simple hand-crafted Concept mode and an AI-enhanced Full Game mode with active health bars, persistent scoreboards. Future versions to include team preview, but for now you'll have to try your luck!
